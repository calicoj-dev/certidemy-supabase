# De facto vocabulary -- phrases used as terms, not declared as terms

Each recurs in four or more rows of the curriculum with **no concept behind it**.
The glossary is `concepts`: a `{glossary=<slug>}` annotation resolves to a concept
slug, so a phrase with no concept is one the curriculum uses as though it were a
defined term while not declaring it.

For each, exactly one of two things is true and neither is the current state:

- **ADD** -- it is a term the exam should test, and it wants a concept; or
- **STOP** -- it is a clause fragment reused out of habit, and it should be
  written differently each time it appears, in our own words.

> An auditor taught vocabulary the exam does not test has been taught the wrong exam.

**1 of the 20 phrases recurring in four or more rows ARE declared**
and are excluded below, listed at the end so the exclusion is visible rather than
silent.
