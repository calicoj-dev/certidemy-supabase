# Below-floor runs of ISO text used in more than one row

Report only. Nothing refuses on this. Attributed quotation excluded.

A run appearing once is incidental collision -- the distribution decays smoothly
and shows no cliff, which is why the floor stays at 10. A run appearing in several
rows is different in kind: recurrence is evidence of carrying that length cannot
supply.

**Three explanations, needing opposite responses**, and only reading separates them:

- **our sentence, reused** -- one sentence of ours pasted across lessons that happens
  to collide with the standard. A style problem, not a leak.
- **a term of art** -- a phrase our prose must contain to teach at all.
- **a carried quotation** -- the standard's wording, lifted once and spread.

The sentence around each occurrence is printed so the three can be told apart.
