## Two classes, and only one is a question about us

**Worked examples (8 rows).** Fictional audit findings, sampling descriptions, criterion
/ evidence blocks. Our own words inside a blockquote. They expand like any prose and
there is nothing to answer for.

**ISO clause text (13 rows).** The translation renders more of the standard than the
English does. **Six are serving.**

## What it is not

It is **not** the generator inventing quotation. Every one of these has a substantial
English quotation already — the translation is longer, not new. The English quotes 19, 22,
44, 46, 65, 74, 81, 82, 145 words in these rows; the surplus rides on top of an existing
blockquote.

## What it probably is, and the one thing that would settle it

The likeliest reading is that **the translation is faithful to an English that has since
been trimmed by hand**, in edits that predate this programme and left no script — §2's
named blind spot, exercised. The alternative is that the translator expanded a quotation
while translating it, which is the same class as the accent batch: one generation run
doing something nobody asked for.

**The two are distinguishable by one thing nobody can do here: scoring the Spanish
against a Spanish edition of the standard.** The leak index is English-only, so a
translated reproduction scores zero by construction. That gap is already the largest of
the three CLAUDE.md records, and this is the first time it has cost a specific answer
about specific rows.
