# The declared splices

Every span replacement declared by an apply script, with the full `to`
sentence and whether it is in the live English today. Read-only, generated by
`scripts/build-splice-file.mjs`.

**Four states, because "not found" was three different facts.** An anchor can be
absent because a later declared edit rewrote the passage (SUPERSEDED — the splice
landed and was then legitimately replaced); absent while MOST OF IT is present
(PARTIAL — one half of a two-part edit landed); or absent with nothing accounting
for it (UNLOCATED). Only the last two are questions, and PARTIAL is the state that
found something: see `ANCHOR-SAMPLE.md`.
