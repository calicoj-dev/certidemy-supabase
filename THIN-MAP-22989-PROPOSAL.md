## The proposal, and it is narrower than the list

**Promote four: 5.19.1, 5.19.2.1, 5.19.3.1, 5.19.5.1.** Those are the four "General" entries that define the
role FAMILIES — provider, producer, partner, and the overview naming all of them. Task 1.2 is about
determining which role an organization holds; the family is the examinable distinction.

**Leave the sub-role definitions supporting.** *AI platform provider* against *AI service or product
provider* is a distinction inside one family. An item keyed on it would test 22989's taxonomy rather than
42001's requirement, and a distractor can still draw on it — which is what supporting is for.

That takes 1.2 from **3 effective primaries to 7**, clear of the floor of 4.
