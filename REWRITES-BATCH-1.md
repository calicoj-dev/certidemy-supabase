## The four clause-8.2 / 6.1.4 trigger flips

Clause 8.2 (27001) and 6.1.4/8.4 (42001) give **two independent triggers**. The
repairs made them joint. Each lesson's own next line still says *two triggers*.

### 1 · `01-03-the-ai-system-life-cycle` — 42001 clause 8, A.4

**now** · assessments at planned intervals, **and again whenever** a significant change is proposed or occurs

**rewrite** · Clause 8.2 sets two independent triggers for a risk assessment: the planned interval, **or** a significant change being proposed or occurring. Either one is enough on its own.

### 2 · `01-03-the-ai-system-life-cycle` — second span

**now** · performed at planned intervals, **and again whenever** a significant change is proposed or occurs.

**rewrite** · The obligation fires on the planned interval **or** on a significant change — whichever comes first, and neither waits for the other.

### 3 · `02-06-the-ai-system-impact-assessment` — 42001 clause 6.1.4, 8.4

**now** · assessments are performed at planned intervals, **and again whenever** a significant change is proposed

**rewrite** · Clause 8.4 requires the impact assessment to be performed at planned intervals **or** when a significant change is proposed or occurs. Two triggers, not a sequence.

### 4 · `03-06-data-for-ai-systems` — 42001 A.7 data controls

**This one changed the meaning, not just the register.** Three alternatives
became two joint requirements.

**was** · known **or** potential biases **or** other systematic errors
**now** · biases known **or** suspected, **and** other systematic errors

**rewrite** · A.7 asks the organization to look for biases that are known, biases that are merely potential, **or** other systematic errors — any one of the three is in scope, and none of them depends on another being present.

## The `available` / `retained` collapse — the sharpest class

ISO MSS drafting distinguishes *shall be **available** as documented
information* from *shall be **retained** as documented information*. Every
substitution below collapses an examinable distinction: an auditor taught
*kept* looks for retention where the clause asks for availability.

### 5 · `03-03-documented-information` — 42001 clause 7.5.3

**now** · is **on hand** and fit to use wherever and whenever it is needed

**rewrite** · Clause 7.5.3 requires documented information to be **available** — obtainable where and when it is needed, and fit to use when it arrives. Availability is not the same obligation as retention, and 7.5.3 asks for the first.

### 6 · `03-04-operational-planning-and-control` — 42001 clause 8.1

**now** · be **kept** to whatever extent gives confidence that the processes

**rewrite** · Clause 8.1 requires documented information to be **available** to the extent needed for confidence that the processes were carried out as planned. The test is whether it can be produced, not whether it was archived.

## Quotation fidelity — text presented as the clause's words

### 7 · `03-01-resources-and-competence` — 42001 clause 7.2

**now** · are competent, grounded in **suitable** education, training or experience

**rewrite** · Clause 7.2 asks the organization to establish competence on the basis of **appropriate** education, training or experience — appropriate to the work in question, which is what makes it auditable against a role rather than against a qualification.

### 8 · `03-02-awareness-and-communication` — 42001 clause 7.3

**`shall` became `must`.** The whole curriculum teaches shall = requirement,
should = recommendation; substituting *must* inside quoted text blurs the exact
distinction the lesson exists to teach.

**now** · Anyone working under the organization's control **must** be aware of:

**rewrite** · Clause 7.3 places a requirement — **shall** — on the organization: persons doing work under its control are to be made aware of the items listed below.

### 9 · `03-02-awareness-and-communication` — 42001 clause 7.4

**now** · **work out which** internal and external communications **bear on** the AI management system

**rewrite** · Clause 7.4 requires the organization to **determine** the internal and external communications **relevant to** the AI management system — determine is the clause's verb and it implies a recorded decision, not an impression.

### 10 · `03-03-documented-information` — 42001 clause 7.5.3

**now** · Documented information **that came from outside**, where the organization **has decided it is needed** to plan and run the AI management system

**rewrite** · Clause 7.5.3 covers documented information **of external origin** that the organization has **determined to be necessary** for planning and operating the AI management system. External origin and a recorded determination are both part of what brings a document into scope.

### 11 · `03-04-operational-planning-and-control` — 42001 clause 8.1

**now** · plan, **put in place** and control whatever processes are needed

**rewrite** · Clause 8.1 requires the organization to plan, **implement** and control the processes needed to meet its requirements and to carry out the clause 6 actions. Implement is the clause's word and it is the middle of three distinct obligations.

### 12 · `04-01-annex-a-structure` — 42001 Annex A intro

**now** · **Not every listed control objective and control must be used**, and an organization **may** design and implement its own.

**rewrite** · Annex A is a reference, not a checklist: the organization is **not required** to use every control objective and control listed, and it **can** design and implement controls of its own. The permission is real and the annex says so in its own opening.

*(Logically this one is sound — not-all and not-every are identical. It is here
because it sits inside text introduced as the annex's words, and `required` →
`must` moves a reserved term.)*

### 13 · `05-01-aims-monitoring-and-measurement` — 42001 clause 9.1

**now** · **What to monitor and measure. Which methods** to use … **so far as each applies**, so that the results are valid.

**rewrite** · Clause 9.1 requires the organization to determine four things: **what needs to be monitored and measured**; **the methods** for monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation, **as applicable**, to ensure valid results; **when** monitoring and measurement are performed; and **when** the results are analysed and evaluated.

### 14 · `05-02-aims-internal-audit` — 42001 clause 9.2.2

**now** · conducted so that the process **stays objective and impartial**

**rewrite** · Clause 9.2.2 b) requires the organization to select auditors and conduct audits so as to **ensure the objectivity and impartiality of the audit process**. Objectivity and impartiality are properties of the process the organization must secure, not dispositions of the individual auditor.

## The founding case

### 15 · `isms-ia-04-02-demonstrated-not-stated` — 27001 clause 5.2 g)

Also live in AIMS-IA's equivalent, and faithfully carried into both
translations.

**now** · - g) be **open to interested parties where appropriate**

**rewrite** · - g) clause 5.2 g) requires the policy to be **available to** interested parties, as appropriate — furnished on request where it is appropriate to do so, which is a narrower obligation than publishing it.

**And the lesson currently contradicts itself two sentences later**, in all
three languages: the explanation beneath still reads *"Available to interested
parties as appropriate is conditional and passive"*. Fixing the quote resolves
the contradiction rather than creating one.
